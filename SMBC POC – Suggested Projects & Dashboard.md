SMBC POC – Suggested Projects & Dashboard 

1. Suggested Projects 

Use 4 projects for the POC. Keep them realistic but simple. 

Global Transaction Banking Platform Modernization 

Status: 🔴 Red 

Example issues: UAT delays, critical defects, integration issues, budget variance 

Capital Markets Technology Transformation 

Status: 🟠 Amber 

Example issues: requirements pending, testing delays, market-data dependency 

Global Digital Platform Enhancement 

Status: 🟢 Green 

Example issues: minor risks, normal development/release activities 

Regulatory & Risk Reporting Transformation 

Status: 🟠 Amber 

Example issues: data-quality gaps, reporting requirements, testing delays 

These should be treated as POC/demo project names and not presented as actual SMBC projects.

List the top 3–4 risks. 

AI Project Summary 

Example:

5. Cross-Source Intelligence 

This should be the key differentiator of the POC. 

Bring together: 

Jira – defects, stories, project status 

Email – risks, escalations, action items 

Documents – project reviews, plans, reports 

Example: 

Jira: UAT completion at 72% 
Email: Business team raised UAT concerns 
Project Review: UAT delayed by 2 weeks 

AI Insight: 

UAT delay is an emerging high-priority risk based on information found across Jira, project communications and project review material. 

 

6. Unattended Actions 

Show a simple list: 

Action 

Owner 

Due Date 

Status 

Source 

Example: 

Resolve API defect – Technology Team – Overdue 

Confirm UAT plan – Business Team – Overdue 

Review infrastructure cost – Infrastructure Team – Open 

AI should be able to identify: 

Actions that have remained unresolved or repeatedly appeared in project updates. 

 

7. AI Queries 

Keep a small set of predefined questions: 

Why is this project at risk? 

What are the top unresolved issues? 

What changed this week? 

Which actions are overdue? 

What should the PMO focus on? 

Are there risks mentioned in emails that are not in Jira? 

Which projects require PMO intervention? 

 

8. Executive Overview 

Add a simple Generate Executive Summary option. 

The output should cover: 

Overall portfolio health 

Projects requiring attention 

Top risks 

Budget concerns 

Schedule concerns 

Overdue actions 

Recommended PMO actions 

 

9. Recommended POC Screens 

Keep the POC limited to 4 main screens: 

1. AI PMO Overview 
Portfolio health + PMO attention 

2. Project Overview 
Project health + risks + budget + schedule + actions 

3. Cross-Source Intelligence 
Jira + Email + Documents → AI insights 

4. Executive Summary 
AI-generated portfolio/project summary 

Guiding Principle 

Don't build another PMO dashboard. Demonstrate how AI can bring together Jira, emails and project documents to identify what is going wrong, explain why, and tell the PMO what needs attention.
